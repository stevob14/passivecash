## 🚀 Features & Tools

### 🧮 Interactive Financial Calculators
- **Compound Interest Calculator**: Project long-term investment growth with variable compounding frequencies (daily, monthly, quarterly, annual) and interactive visual charts.
- **Portfolio Rebalancer**: Dynamically calculate exact dollar amounts needed to rebalance multi-asset portfolios back to target allocations.
- **CPI Inflation Calculator**: Calculate purchasing power changes across historical years (1913–present) powered by automated monthly Bureau of Labor Statistics (BLS) CPI-U updates.
- **Mortgage Payment Calculator**: Estimate monthly principal, interest, property taxes, insurance, and private mortgage insurance (PMI).

### 📚 Comprehensive Passive Income Guides
In-depth walkthroughs covering core wealth-building and cash flow vehicles:
- **Dividend Growth Investing & DRIP**: Compounding income with dividend-paying stocks and ETFs.
- **High-Yield Savings Accounts (HYSA)**: Maximizing liquid cash reserves with competitive yields.
- **Treasury Securities & TIPS**: Protecting capital against inflation with government-backed paper.
- **Crypto & Web3 Yield**: CeFi staking, decentralized lending protocols, and covered call crypto ETFs.
- **Real Estate & REITs**: Generating hands-off property income through publicly traded real estate investment trusts.
- **Options Selling Strategies**: Systematic income generation via covered calls and cash-secured puts.
- **0% APR Credit Card Arbitrage**: Low-risk capital deployment strategies during promotional periods.

### 📈 Live Market Overview
- Real-time major market indices tracking (S&P 500, Nasdaq, Dow Jones).
- Direct access to trending financial market news and due diligence discussions.

- **Frontend**: HTML5 semantic markup, CSS3 (responsive grid/flexbox dark mode), Vanilla JavaScript (ES6+), jQuery
- **Data Visualization**: Chart.js
- **Automation**: GitHub Actions & Python workflow for monthly Bureau of Labor Statistics (BLS) CPI data updates
- **Hosting & CDN**: GitHub Pages with custom domain and Cloudflare DNS
